The Fourth Day Chat

Living the Fourth Day

The New Heart weekend is only the beginning. The “Fourth Day” is every day after the weekend where we live out the new heart God has given us.

During the three days of New Heart, we encounter the cross, forgiveness, and the Father’s embrace. Tears are shed, walls come down, and long-buried wounds are brought into the light of Jesus.

But the Fourth Day begins when the retreat ends. It is Monday morning back at work. It is the quiet kitchen where you wash dishes after a long day. It is the commute, the classroom, the hidden places where no one else sees but the Lord.

On the Fourth Day, we learn to return again and again to the cross. We choose to forgive, sometimes not just once, but seventy times seven. We let the Father speak His delight over us not just in a session, but in the middle of our real, messy lives.

Our prayer is that this newsletter would help you remember that you are part of a global family of “Fourth Day” disciples—from Korea to Thailand, from Vietnam to Israel, from the U.S. and beyond. May every day after New Heart be filled with His love, His mercy, and fresh courage to keep saying “yes” to His heart.

— New Heart Ministry Editorial Team

Ariel — From Orthodox Judaism to Jesus

Growing up in an Orthodox Jewish home, Ariel’s life was shaped by tradition and strict observance. Yet there was a deep hunger in her heart for the living God.

Through New Heart, the Holy Spirit gently dismantled walls of fear and opened her to encounter Jesus as Messiah, Savior, and Bridegroom. She left the weekend with a new heart and a bold love for her family and nation.

Choi — A Heart Healed from Shame

Choi carried a quiet shame from past failures and the heavy weight of family expectations. She often felt she was never “enough” — not holy enough, not strong enough, not worthy enough of love.

At New Heart, God named her “beloved” and washed over her with forgiveness and honor. In times of worship and ministry, the Lord exposed lies of condemnation and replaced them with His truth. Choi left with a lighter heart, standing taller in the identity of a treasured daughter.

Han — From Striving to Rest

Han’s life was driven by performance and ministry work. She measured her value through what she did for God and others. Burnout, exhaustion, and hidden disappointment accumulated in her heart.

Through the cross message and the gentle work of the Holy Spirit, Han encountered Jesus who said, “You are loved apart from what you do.” The Lord invited her into rest, not as laziness, but as a place of trust. Serving is now an overflow of His love, not a way to earn it.

Keziah — Encountering the Father’s Love

Keziah had walked with the Lord for years yet carried layers of rejection and striving in her heart. She longed to know God’s love not only in her mind but deep in her identity.

During New Heart, she wept as the Father’s love was spoken over her. Scriptures and prophetic words pierced through old fears and lies. Years of rejection were replaced with the assurance, “You are My daughter in whom I delight.” She returned home anchored in a new freedom and security in His love.

Parichat — 18 Years of Pain Transformed

I, Parichat, would like to express my heartfelt thanks to Spiritual Mother Pastor Mayuri, Pastor Sarah, and Pastor Matthew for their unwavering guidance, care, and love from the moment we arrived in Korea until the final day of the mission.

Thank you for the precious experiences and profound blessings I received through my involvement with the “New Heart” mission in Korea.

I was deeply impressed by every aspect of the “New Heart” mission, including: the warm transportation arrangement to pick up our team, the loving welcome through songs of praise, the joyful meal team filled with laughter, the worship team overflowing with the Holy Spirit, and the prayer team that continually covered us throughout the mission.

Thank you for every testimony and sermon shared by the pastors and the brothers and sisters who had previously gone through the New Heart mission. Your sharing helped us understand even more that no matter how complex or painful the situation may be, God still reigns. He continually upholds our hearts and spirits, keeping us steadfast in His righteousness now and forever.

I received deep inner restoration and have made a firm decision to serve and draw even closer to God. I also chose to forgive my ex-husband for everything in the past and even in the present. The accusations and hatred that I carried for many years have now transformed into something new that leads me to pray even more. I have truly experienced transformation in both my heart and my spirit.

Before joining the mission, I had carried pain in my heart for more than 18 years. But now, that burden has become so much lighter, and I can truly feel that I am no longer carrying it the way I once did.

“NEW 54” has been a great blessing and a transformative experience for both my heart and my spirit. I also desire to become a blessing to the brothers and sisters who will join the next generation of the New Heart mission.

May the Lord strengthen all the pastors and pour out abundant blessings on every ministry you lead, so that great fruit will come forth and flow into the lives of many people.

Ji — Healing from Father Wounds

As Ji served as a coordinator for New Heart Kids, she also carried unhealed memories with her earthly father. In times of worship and quiet reflection, the Lord gently brought these memories to the surface and met her there.

As she faced painful moments with honesty, the Lord revealed Himself as a gentle, safe, and faithful Abba. Her tears became worship, and the places of fear and distance were transformed into intimacy and trust. Ji left with a new confidence that she is cherished by a perfect Father.

Kyungjin — Restored in Body and Soul

Kyungjin came to New Heart exhausted in body and soul. Balancing ministry, family, and the hidden pressures of life had left her worn down in ways she could no longer ignore.

Through worship, teaching, and personal prayer, the Lord restored strength and joy. He refreshed her physically, emotionally, and spiritually. She even experienced tangible healing in her body. Kyungjin returned home with renewed hope, carrying a deeper assurance that Jesus is both Healer and Shepherd for her whole life.
