Editorial

Compelled by the Fire: The Fourth Day

New Heart Ministry recently celebrated its 54th gathering in Korea, marking nearly two decades of God's undeniable grace across nations, including Cambodia, Vietnam, China, Ethiopia, Israel, and Korea. This movement is a divine summons igniting the promise of a New Heart (Ezekiel 36:26) in every generation and weaving us into one fierce, global Kingdom Family. Our commitment is comprehensive, extending to the New Heart Kids programs, which underscore our conviction that children are critical and active participants today, ensuring a legacy of passion that will sustain the movement for years to come.

The Fourth Day: Our Unstoppable Mandate

Our three-day gatherings are a deep immersion—a holy soaking in God's love and transformative power, but this is the preparation, not the end; we are utterly compelled to live the Fourth Day, which is the immediate, daily application of our radical, transformed hearts and our unstoppable mandate to reject spiritual stagnation and pursue sustained maturity in God's goodness and grace by remaining connected, to hold nothing back and serve in unified purpose (Acts 2) because isolation is not an option and our strength is in our unbreakable family covenant spanning continents, and to constantly commit to this ultimate purpose of raising a people of fire and fulfilling the global expansion God promised in Isaiah 54.

This Global Newsletter

As God's vision for New Heart Ministry continues to sharpen with undeniable clarity, we sense His urgent desire not only to document the mighty works and abundant grace flowing but also for us, the Family, to become more intimately intertwined and nurtured; hence, this quarterly Global Newsletter for the New Heart Family is a necessity, not an option, serving as the intentional tool designed to bridge the miles and cultures across the world to nurture global connections and ensure we care for one another as one body, to share the undeniable evidence of God's faithfulness from every region, solidifying our collective faith, and to keep our focus sharp, reminding us of our compulsion to live out the Fourth Day together and fostering sustained growth and maturity until the next gathering.

We must step forward. The Kingdom Family is not a passive recipient of grace, but an active force compelled by an unwavering vision. By His strength, we will fulfill every declaration over this ministry.

Ariel — From Orthodox Judaism to Jesus

My sister's profound experience with New Heart 52 in Israel had a deep impact on me. Witnessing her transformation and her confession of faith in Jesus to our mother inspired me to attend New Heart in the USA in May 2025.

I was born into a devout Orthodox Jewish family, alongside my sister and our triplet brother. Our upbringing was steeped in the traditions and teachings of our faith, and for many years, this was the foundation of my religion. However, as I grew older, a sense of unease began to stir within me. Despite my fervent prayers and commitment to our religious practices, I found myself yearning for a deeper connection, a more profound experience of God's peace and love, which I felt was lacking.

This spiritual yearning led me to seek guidance, and I prayed earnestly for God to lead me to the truth. About six years ago, my journey took a transformative turn. I began to research Jesus, approaching the subject with an open mind and a sincere desire to understand. What I discovered during this period of intense study and reflection was nothing short of revelatory.

I delved into the prophecies of the Hebrew Bible and was astonished to see how clearly they pointed to Jesus as the long-awaited Messiah. The intricate details, the historical accuracy, and the profound theological implications of these prophecies resonated deeply within me. It was a profound awakening, confirming a truth that had been quietly stirring in my heart. This journey of discovery solidified my belief in Jesus, forever changing my spiritual path.

Getting permission to leave Israel during my army service, especially with the ongoing war, was challenging. We booked the flight, prayed earnestly, and by God's grace, I received permission just one day before I departed for the USA.

The New Heart program was a period of immense grace and personal change. Over three days, I grew closer to God by spending undistracted time with Him, a profoundly enriching experience. I am grateful to God for transforming my life and giving me a new heart during New Heart 53.

Choi Eun-Young — Called Back to the Fight

Gratitude: First, I cannot forget the spiritual leaders who delivered the Word, the praise team who gave their body and soul, the Palanca team who stayed up all night making many things with devotion, those who shared love through food and snacks, the general affairs team who handled all the troublesome work behind the scenes so the gathering could proceed, and above all, the Royo members who shared their entire lives with tears and love, and each tearful prayer offered on their knees during the foot-washing ceremony. I am truly grateful. I received a profound love that one cannot receive in life.

Situation Before Going: I received an invitation to attend this retreat from a pastor's wife a year ago. She had been praying for me for over a year. Then this year, I received another invitation to attend the retreat. I strongly felt God inviting me to this gathering through the hands of a co-worker.

Grace Received: As a mother of three children and a working mom, my daily life was like a battlefield. I was exhausted in body and mind, and I was living day by day without any strength, just barely holding on. Then I came to the New Heart gathering. From the first day, the Word of God began to pound my heart. The praise was so passionate that it felt like the Holy Spirit was pouring down like fire. I wept and repented during every prayer time. I realized how far I had drifted from God and how much I had been relying on my own strength. I made a decision to live the rest of my life for God's Kingdom, not for my own comfort and success. I am so grateful to God for calling me back to the fight. I will not forget this grace and will live as a soldier of Christ.

Han Ye-Sul — A Renewed Commitment

My journey with New Heart began years ago, and serving as a team member for NH Korea #54 was a full-circle moment. Seeing the candidates' faces transform from burden to freedom reminded me of my own first experience.

Serving is not always easy. It requires sacrifice, patience, and a heart that is willing to put others first. There were moments of physical exhaustion and spiritual battle. But in those moments, God's grace was sufficient. He sustained me and gave me the strength to continue.

The most rewarding part was witnessing the Holy Spirit at work. It was a privilege to pray with the candidates, to listen to their stories, and to see them encounter God in a fresh and powerful way. Their testimonies of healing and restoration were a testament to God's faithfulness.

Through this experience, my own commitment to the Lord has been renewed. I am reminded that we are called to be vessels of His love and grace. I am excited to see how God will continue to use the New Heart ministry to touch lives and transform hearts around the world. I am committed to living out the "Fourth Day" with a heart full of gratitude and a spirit of service.

Keziah — Finding True Belonging

Coming from Vietnam to attend New Heart #14 was a step of faith. I had heard amazing things about the ministry, but I didn't know what to expect. From the moment I arrived, I felt a sense of belonging that I had never experienced before.

The love and acceptance I received from the team and the other candidates were overwhelming. It was a safe space to share my struggles, my doubts, and my fears. I realized that I was not alone in my journey.

During the sessions, God spoke to me in a very personal way. He revealed the lies that I had been believing about myself and replaced them with His truth. I learned that I am a beloved child of God, chosen and precious in His sight.

The times of worship and prayer were incredibly powerful. I felt the presence of the Holy Spirit in a tangible way. I experienced deep healing and liberation from past wounds. I left the gathering with a new sense of identity and purpose. I am so grateful for the New Heart family and for the transformational work that God is doing in my life.

Parichat — Bridging Nations with Love

Serving on the Thailand Team for NH Korea #54 was a unique and enriching experience. It was a beautiful picture of the global body of Christ coming together to serve and to love.

Despite the language and cultural barriers, we were united in one Spirit and one purpose. We learned so much from each other and were blessed by the unique gifts and perspectives that each person brought to the team.

Seeing the Korean candidates encounter God's love was a powerful reminder that His grace transcends all boundaries. It was a privilege to be a part of their journey and to witness the transformation in their lives.

This experience has deepened my love for the global church and has given me a greater passion for missions. I am excited to see how God will continue to use the New Heart ministry to build bridges between nations and to spread His love to the ends of the earth. I am committed to being a bridge-builder and a carrier of His love wherever I go.

Ji Yeowoon — Serving the Next Generation

Being a coordinator for New Heart Kids Korea #2 was a challenging but incredibly rewarding experience. It was a joy to see the children's faces light up as they learned about God's love and participated in the activities.

We wanted to create a fun and engaging environment where the children could encounter God in a way that was relevant to their age and understanding. We used songs, games, crafts, and Bible stories to teach them about God's character and His plan for their lives.

It was amazing to see how quickly the children grasped spiritual truths. Their faith was simple and pure, and it was a reminder to me of the importance of having a childlike faith.

I am so grateful for the opportunity to invest in the next generation. I believe that these children are the future leaders of the church, and it is our responsibility to nurture their faith and to equip them to serve God. I am excited to see what God has in store for them.

Kyungjin (Maria) Cha — A Child's Pure Faith

Attending New Heart Kids USA #1 was so much fun! I made new friends and learned so many new things about Jesus. My favorite part was the worship time. I loved singing and dancing for God.

We also learned about how much God loves us and how He wants us to love others. We did cool crafts that helped us remember the Bible stories. The teachers were really nice and they made everything fun.

One day, we prayed for each other, and I felt God's love in my heart. I know that Jesus is my best friend and that He is always with me. I want to tell my friends about Jesus so they can know Him too.

I can't wait to go back to New Heart Kids again! It was the best weekend ever.
