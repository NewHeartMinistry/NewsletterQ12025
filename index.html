The Fourth Day Chat

Living the Fourth Day

The New Heart weekend is only the beginning. The “Fourth Day” is every day after the weekend where we walk out the new heart God has given us in the ordinary moments of life.

Three Days and a Lifetime

During the three days of New Heart, we encounter the cross, forgiveness, and the Father’s embrace. The Fourth Day is where these truths become a lifestyle of surrender, love, and obedience.

A Global Family Story

From Korea to Israel, from Thailand to the U.S., God is weaving together a global family of New Heart alumni who carry His healing, freedom, and love into their churches, homes, and nations.

May every Fourth Day be filled with the fire of His love, the tenderness of His mercy, and the courage to keep saying “yes” to His heart.

Ariel — From Orthodox Judaism to Jesus

Growing up in an Orthodox Jewish home, Ariel’s life was shaped by tradition and strict observance. Yet there was a deep hunger in her heart for the living God.

Through New Heart, the Holy Spirit gently dismantled walls of fear and opened her to encounter Jesus as Messiah, Savior, and Bridegroom. She left the weekend with a new heart and a bold love for her family and nation.

Choi — A Heart Healed from Shame

Choi carried a quiet shame from past failures and family expectations. At New Heart, God named her “beloved” and washed over her with forgiveness and honor.

Han — From Striving to Rest

Han’s life was driven by performance and ministry work. Through the cross message, Han encountered Jesus who said, “You are loved apart from what you do.”

Keziah — Encountering the Father’s Love

Keziah wept as the Father’s love was spoken over her. Years of rejection were replaced with the assurance, “You are My daughter in whom I delight.”

Parichat — Freedom from Fear

The Lord gently confronted Parichat’s lifelong fear and showed her that perfect love casts out fear. She left with a courage she had never known.

Ji — Healing from Father Wounds

As Ji faced painful memories with her earthly father, the Lord revealed Himself as a gentle, safe, and faithful Abba. Her tears became worship.

Kyungjin — Restored in Body and Soul

Kyungjin came to New Heart exhausted in body and soul. Through worship and prayer, the Lord restored strength and joy, and even brought physical healing.
